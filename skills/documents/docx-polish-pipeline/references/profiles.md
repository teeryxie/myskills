# Profiles

## `formal-business`

Use for customer-facing or executive-facing deliverables.

Characteristics:

- stronger title and heading hierarchy
- restrained blue-gray accent colors
- table-first presentation for key facts
- concise conclusions and explicit scope language

## `formal-report`

Use for internal summaries, progress reports, implementation notes, or structured working documents.

Characteristics:

- report-style section flow
- less ceremonial than business documents
- balanced paragraphs and tables
- moderate emphasis on headings over cover treatment

## `technical-clean`

Use for technical documentation, engineering notes, method descriptions, and evidence-heavy documents.

Characteristics:

- prioritize density and clarity
- reduce decorative emphasis
- keep tables compact
- avoid overly dramatic cover formatting

## Profile Selection Rule

If the user does not specify a profile, default to `formal-business` for anything that may be delivered outside the immediate working group. Default to `formal-report` for internal work products with moderate polish needs.
